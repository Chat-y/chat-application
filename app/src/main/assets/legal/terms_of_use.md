# Chaty Terms of Use

REVIEW DRAFT - version 0.3-draft, 15 November 2026.
Not legally reviewed, not published and not an active acceptance requirement. This local draft is available for review in the app. The final operator identity, contact details, age requirements, governing law and dispute process must be confirmed before publication. Nothing here limits rights that the law does not allow a service to limit.

## 1. About Chaty
Chaty provides messaging, media sharing, voice and video calls, and workspace features. Features depend on your app version, permissions, network and workspace settings. A workspace may be managed by its own administrator. This draft does not identify an unverified company as the legal contracting party.

## 2. Your account and security
Use registration information you are entitled to use. Keep your password and email verification codes private. Do not share another person's account or impersonate anyone. Review devices and security settings if you think an account is being used without permission. Android's password provider may offer to save a password after a successful sign-in; you choose whether to accept.

## 3. Respect other people
Do not use Chaty to threaten, harass, exploit or unlawfully discriminate against people; distribute illegal sexual content or material that endangers children; violate privacy or intellectual-property rights; send malware or deceptive links; impersonate people or organizations; or run spam, fraud or unauthorized access attempts. Do not bypass security, access controls or another person's blocks.

## 4. Your content
You retain the rights you hold in the content you share. A final agreement should give the service only the permission reasonably needed to transmit, store and display that content as you direct. Share content only when you have the necessary rights and consent. Recipients may keep copies or share what they receive; deleting something on your device cannot guarantee deletion of another person's copies.

## 5. Workspaces, reporting and restrictions
Workspace administrators may control membership, registration and available features. Use the app's blocking and reporting controls when available. A final policy must explain who reviews reports, how to appeal restrictions and how urgent safety reports are handled. Those operational commitments have not been verified, so this draft does not promise a response time or monitoring service.

## 6. Optional permissions and features
Some features need Android permissions or special access. Camera and microphone access support capture and calls. Notification, overlay and accessibility access for the optional cross-app island are separate from ordinary messaging. Grant only the access you want and use Android Settings to revoke it. A permission does not guarantee that an Android background feature will remain available at all times.

## 7. Availability and emergencies
Networks, devices and service failures can delay messages or calls. Do not rely on Chaty as your only way to contact emergency services. A final agreement must state any lawful availability, warranty and liability terms. This draft does not promise uninterrupted service or waive non-waivable consumer rights.

## 8. Privacy, account closure and updates
Read the draft Privacy Policy for the data categories and open questions under review. Final retention periods, account-deletion handling and backup cleanup must be confirmed before publication. A final policy should explain how material changes are announced and when renewed acceptance is needed. This draft alone does not activate a new consent gate or change an account's existing rights.

## 9. Age, legal jurisdiction and contact
No minimum age, legal entity, governing law or support address is invented in this draft. These must be supplied and reviewed for the places where the service is offered. Until then, do not treat this document as a published agreement or legal advice.
